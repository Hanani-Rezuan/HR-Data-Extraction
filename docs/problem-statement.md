# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Human Resource Administrator, Employees, Organization

## What exactly is going wrong?
Manual Data-entry is time consuming, Repetitive Document Processing, Higher Risk Of Human Error

## Why does it matter?
Slows down Human Resource operations, Increase administrator workload, Affect data accuracy and consistency 

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Human Resource Administrator are required to manually process and key in enter employee information from alot of documents. This time consuming, repetitive document processing and will lead to higher risk of human error. As a result, Human Resource operations process will becomes slower, increases administrator workload and affect data accuracy and consistency.
